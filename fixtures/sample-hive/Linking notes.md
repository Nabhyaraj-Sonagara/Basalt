# Linking notes

Write `[[Note name]]` to link. Add a heading with `[[Welcome#What to try]]` or a block with `[[Linking notes#^core-idea]]`.

Links are the core idea: a note gains meaning from what points at it. ^core-idea

Embeds pull content in place:

![[Welcome#What to try]]

Mentions of Welcome without brackets show up as unlinked mentions.
