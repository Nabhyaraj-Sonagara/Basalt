# Welcome to your hive

A **hive** is a folder of Markdown notes. Basalt keeps them as plain files, so they stay yours.

Link notes with double brackets: [[Linking notes]]. Every link shows up as a backlink on the other side.
See the [[Projects/Basalt launch|launch plan]] for a longer example, and [[Ideas]] for a note that doesn't exist yet.

> [!tip] Open anything fast
> Press Ctrl+O to jump to a note, or Ctrl+P for every command.
